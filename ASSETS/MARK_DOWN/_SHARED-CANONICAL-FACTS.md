## 6. Non-negotiable architectural rules

These are settled and no agent may relitigate them without an ADR (§8).

**6.1 — Claims never live in code.** A component renders `<ClaimText id="…" />` resolved from `content/claims-register.json`. Free-text claim copy in JSX or MDX fails CI.

**6.2 — HCP-tier content is gated server-side.** Never CSS-hidden, never client-filtered. Gated content must be absent from the public HTML payload.

**6.3 — The disease-education firewall.** Condition pages (education) carry no product claim and no product CTA in body copy. Product pages name the condition once, factually. One neutral, visually separated bridge module links them. Adjacency is what converts lawful information into an unlawful advertisement.

**6.4 — Every product route renders the §3 disclosure block.**

**6.5 — Real URLs for products and sectors.** Client-side-only tabs on a single URL are prohibited; see §7.4.

**6.6 — No AI-generated human figures anywhere**, and no AI generation of the product itself or its label. AI generates the environment around a real product render. (ASCI AI guidelines, May 2026 — AI-generated authority figures implying medical expertise are *prohibited*, not merely label-required.)

**6.7 — No claim renders without an evidence grade and at least one citation.**

## 7. Arbitrated conflicts — rulings

Source documents disagree. These are the rulings. Each carries the reasoning so agents can apply the same logic to new cases.

### 7.1 BRESTIL — PRD v2.0 is wrong; do not publish

PRD v2.0 proposes a "compliant" rewrite: *"Nourishing massage roll-on for breast wellness; toning and nourishing."*

**Ruling: reject.** DMR Act 1954 **Schedule item 21** prohibits advertising for *"forms and structure of the female bust"*, and Schedule J item 19 repeats it as *"form, structure of breast"*. The Schedule names the **body part**, not merely the verb. Softening "increases breast size" to "breast toning" does not exit the Schedule — a topical product advertised for the form or structure of the bust is within it regardless of adjective. PRD v2.0's own basis column concedes the prohibition, then proposes copy that remains inside it.

Additional grounds: the leaflet claims against **mastitis** and **mastalgia**. Mastitis requires antibiotics; positioning a massage oil against it is a genuine patient-safety risk, independent of advertising law.

**Action:** omit from launch. If a listing is commercially unavoidable, the maximum is name + composition + "consult your physician", with **zero** benefit, indication, mechanism or imagery — which is commercially pointless. Obtain SKU-specific legal advice before any public listing.

### 7.2 Lead product — GUANOLACT first for build, ESTROCLEN for marketing

PRD v2.0 makes **Estroclen** the homepage hero (strategic whitespace: no doctor-channel competitor in India for oestrogen balance) and relegates **Guanolact** to a placeholder.

**Ruling: both, for different purposes — this is not actually a conflict.**
- **Estroclen = marketing hero.** The whitespace argument is sound. Accept for the Home flagship carousel and the Women's Health tab.
- **Guanolact = first product page built.** Iron has **pre-existing permitted nutrient-function claims** ("iron contributes to normal formation of haemoglobin", "reduction of tiredness and fatigue"). It is the only SKU where a lawful claim can be written today without new substantiation work. Build it first as the reference implementation of the dossier template, then clone the pattern.

Building the hardest-to-justify product first would block the template on legal review. Building the easiest first unblocks everything else. **Guanolact is therefore promoted from placeholder to full page.**

### 7.3 FERTISCOPE — wrong regulator, hold

Neither PRD addresses this. Fertiscope is a saliva-ferning **mini microscope** — an *in-vitro* diagnostic device for ovulation prediction. It falls under the **Medical Devices Rules 2017 (CDSCO)**, not the FSSAI nutraceutical licence. Its "98% accuracy" claim is a diagnostic-accuracy claim; saliva ferning has poor published concordance with LH surge and the figure is not supportable.

**Ruling:** do not publish until CDSCO licensure is confirmed. Listing an unlicensed diagnostic device alongside foods risks drawing an inspection onto the entire company. If launched later it needs its own template, an intended-use statement, and an explicit *"not a contraceptive; not a substitute for clinical fertility assessment"* warning.

### 7.4 Products routing — real routes, not hash-synced tabs

PRD v2.0 specifies a single `/products` URL with `URL hash sync` across 7 tabs, 6 products.

**Ruling: keep the tab UX exactly; change the addressing.** A URL fragment is not a document — `#women` does not produce an indexable page, a unique `<title>`, a canonical, or per-product `Product`/`FAQPage` structured data. Nine products on one URL is one search result.

Implementation that satisfies both: `/products/[sector]` and `/products/[sector]/[product]` as real prerendered routes; the tab strip becomes prefetched `<Link>`s; navigation is client-side and feels identical to a `useState` tab. Sector-route instances of a product set `rel=canonical` to the product URL. Same treatment for Resources Hub filters.

### 7.5 GSAP Club membership — factual error, remove the budget line

PRD v2.0 states, as a **P0 pre-development blocking gate with a finance owner**: *"GSAP Club membership is a hard pre-development budget requirement: MorphSVG and SplitText plugins used in hero and R&D sticky-stack are not available on free GSAP tier"* and *"ScrollTrigger is a Club plugin for commercial use."*

**Ruling: false. Delete the gate and the budget line.** Webflow made GSAP **100% free on 30 April 2025**, including every former Club plugin — MorphSVG, SplitText, ScrollSmoother, DrawSVG, Inertia — and expanded the standard licence to cover commercial use. ScrollTrigger was never a paid plugin. Verified against gsap.com/pricing, the standard "no charge" licence (effective 30 Apr 2025), and the GSAP GitHub README. The only carve-out is for companies that compete with Webflow, which is irrelevant here.

Open question #13 is **closed**. Blocking gate removed. Cost: ₹0.

### 7.6 Design system — UNRESOLVED, held for AGENT-2

### 7.7 Framework and CMS

- **Next.js:** PRD v2.0 says 14. **Ruling: Next.js 15**, App Router. Same paradigm, current LTS, no migration debt at greenfield.
- **CMS:** PRD v2.0 mandates Sanity.io from day one with a full schema and webhook-driven ISR. Earlier guidance said MDX/JSON first. **Ruling: accept Sanity.** Rationale: a non-technical marketing owner must publish Resources Hub content without a deploy, `claims-register.json` benefits from an approval workflow with named reviewers and an audit trail, and PRD v2.0 has already specified the schema, webhook and ISR revalidation contract in implementable detail. Reversing it now discards finished work for no gain.

### 7.8 Melatonin — regulatory status is live risk

PRD v2.0 sources flag: *"Melatonin reclassification as Schedule H drug pending; affects QuickNap marketing claims."* Independently flagged in `13-CLAIMS-TRANSFORMATION.md §13.3.8`. If melatonin becomes Schedule H, QuickNap cannot be sold or advertised as a nutraceutical at all. **Confirm current status with Peptas Pharma in writing before QuickNap copy is finalised, and re-check before launch.**

### 7.9 Rule 170 AYUSH — check applicability

AYUSH advertising **pre-clearance** was reinstated in August 2024 and applies to any product classified under AYUSH systems. Several Varuta products are multi-herb Ayurvedic formulations (Cystorin is nine classical botanicals). **Determine per SKU whether the licence is FSSAI-nutraceutical or AYUSH.** If AYUSH, advertising requires pre-clearance and the compliance path changes materially. This is an unresolved regulatory question, not a copy question.
