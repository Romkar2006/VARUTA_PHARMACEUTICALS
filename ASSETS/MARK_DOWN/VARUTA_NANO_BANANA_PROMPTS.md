## HOW NANO BANANA PRO ACTUALLY WANTS TO BE PROMPTED

Six elements, in this order, written as natural photographer's language, not a keyword list:
1. **Subject** — the exact product, described precisely
2. **Composition & camera** — framing, angle, lens behavior (speak to it like a photographer, not a spec sheet)
3. **Lighting & color grade** — direction, quality, mood
4. **Background/setting** — what's behind and around the product
5. **Text-in-image** — anything that should render as legible text, always in quotes
6. **Constraints** — what must NOT appear ("no X, no X, no X")

**The single most important line in every prompt below, non-negotiable:** an instruction to preserve the real product's exact appearance from your reference photo — shape, label text, certification badges, batch/expiry info, colors, proportions. Nano Banana Pro will otherwise "helpfully" reinterpret label text, which for a regulated product is not a cosmetic problem, it's a compliance one.

**Upload your real product reference photo(s) with every prompt** — front-face box shot at minimum, blister strip photo as a second reference where relevant (per the 360°-footage photos you've already been capturing).

---

## STRICT PACKAGING INTEGRITY & ZERO-ALTERATION DIRECTIVE

> [!CRITICAL]
> **REGULATORY & BRAND PROTECTION RULE — DO NOT ALTER PACKAGING UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCE**
> 1. **1:1 Physical Preservation**: The generated product packaging MUST BE AN EXACT 100% REPLICA of the uploaded real-world reference image. Do NOT allow the AI to alter, redesign, "improve", modernise, or reinterpret the physical box, label layout, or packaging geometry.
> 2. **Zero Text & Logo Hallucinations**: All brand logos, product names ("GUANOLACT", "QUICKNAP", "CYSTORIN", "ESTROCLEN", "FATEASE-5", "TELAGE", "ERECTER"), Varuta Pharma emblem, subtitle text, dosage counts, active ingredient lists, certification stamps (FSSAI, ISO, GMP), and batch/expiry markings must remain 100% pixel-accurate to the reference photo.
> 3. **Exact Color & Material Fidelity**: Preserve the exact Pantone/hex packaging colors, box surface finish (matte vs. gloss), paper board texture, foil embossing, and blister strip foil patterns as shown in the uploaded reference photography.
> 4. **AI Role Limited to Environment & Lighting**: AI image generation is strictly restricted to rendering stage lighting, camera depth of field, and background studio gradients surrounding the product. **The product box itself must never be mutated.**

## HOW THIS TIES INTO YOUR EXISTING PIPELINE

- Use these Nano Banana Pro outputs as your **Stage-1 hero/gallery images** in the product media pipeline we built earlier — they sit alongside (not instead of) the Claid.ai/Photoroom-cleaned real photos from your 360° footage. **Where the AI generation and real photo disagree on the product's actual appearance, the real photographed packaging ALWAYS wins** — Nano Banana outputs are strictly for studio lighting polish and clean backgrounds, NEVER for modifying or inventing what the product box looks like.
- **Packaging Lock**: Always compare your generated image side-by-side with the reference photograph before publishing. If any text character, logo placement, or box proportion has shifted, re-run with stronger image-weight / reference framing or revert to the real cleaned photograph.
- The hero shots from this set are also what should feed into **Google Flow's Omni mode** from our earlier prompt template, as the reference image for the cinematic "walk-style" video versions.
- Once you've generated all 7 sets, it's worth a final side-by-side pass across the whole catalogue to check the per-product mood shifts (warm/Guanolact, cool/QuickNap, deep/Telage, bright/Fatease-5) read as a coherent family rather than 7 disconnected styles — small grade adjustments may be needed to keep them feeling like one brand.
